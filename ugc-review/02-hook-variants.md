# Hook Variants

First 3 seconds decide the video. Shoot all eight in one sitting — same tee, same
light — and swap them over the identical body. Cheapest A/B test available.

---

**1. Name-first (default)**
> "Okay I bought a plain green t-shirt from a brand called *Feesbees* and I need to talk about it."

**2. Color-led**
> "I need someone to explain why this t-shirt is the exact color of a matcha latte."

**3. Objection-first**
> "This is a plain green t-shirt for [PRICE]. Talk me out of it. …You can't, I already tried."

**4. Anti-hype**
> "This is not a life-changing t-shirt. It's just the only one I've worn four days this week."

**5. Wardrobe-problem**
> "Every t-shirt I own is black, white, or grey. This one broke the streak."

**6. Direct-address**
> "If you're the person who keeps asking where the green shirt is from — it's this."

**7. Skeptic-converted**
> "I ordered this expecting to return it. Cutting the tag off was a whole decision."

**8. Detail-obsessive**
> "The reason I like this shirt is a two-millimeter piece of stitching and I'm going to show you."
> *(pairs with the collar macro shot)*

---

## Hooks to avoid

- "You NEED this shirt" — reads as ad copy instantly
- Anything opening on the price as a bargain — flattens the product into a discount
- Unboxing as the hook — the mailer is not interesting, the color is
- "Let's get into it" / "so I've been testing" — dead air where the hook should be
