## Why these read as real

- Opens with a specific, slightly unflattering personal fact (eleven shirts, ten of them grey)
- Contains genuine criticism — thin product page, shows dirt — which is what separates a review from a testimonial
- Sensory specifics that only ownership produces: collar shape after washing, print texture, sleeve length
- No superlatives, no brand name repeated for SEO, no "highly recommend to anyone looking for…"
