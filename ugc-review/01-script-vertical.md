# Primary Script — 32s Vertical UGC Review

**Format:** 9:16 · handheld · natural light · single creator
**Tone:** talking to a friend, not to a camera. Mild skepticism up front, won over by specifics.
**Delivery note:** let it breathe. One stumble or self-correction left in the cut is worth more than a clean read.

---

### 0:00–0:03 — Hook

> "Okay I bought a plain green t-shirt from a brand called *Feesbees* and I need to talk about it."

`ON-SCREEN: not sponsored, just weirdly obsessed` *(swap to `#ad` if gifted — see README)*
`SHOT: creator mid-sentence, tee already on, slightly off-center framing`

---

### 0:03–0:08 — Why they're skeptical

> "Because the name is ridiculous, the product page is one photo, and I fully expected this to feel like a school uniform."

`SHOT: quick cut — creator holds phone showing the product page`

---

### 0:08–0:14 — The turn

> "And then it showed up and the color is just — that's *matcha*. That's a matcha latte. Nothing I own is this color and now everything I own is wrong."

`SHOT: tee held up to window light, slow tilt so the green shifts`

---

### 0:14–0:22 — Specific proof

> "It's [FABRIC], [GSM], so it's got actual weight to it. Hold it up — you can't see my hand through it. And the collar's still round after a wash, which is genuinely the only thing I care about in a t-shirt."

`SHOT A: hand behind fabric, held to light — opacity proof`
`SHOT B: thumb running the ribbed collar, macro`
`SHOT C: fingernail flick against the chest logo — the print is flat, not plasticky`

---

### 0:22–0:28 — Objection handling

> "Fit's [FIT]. I'm [CREATOR_HEIGHT] and I took a [CREATOR_SIZE] — if you want it boxy, size up, it's not one of those tees that's secretly a crop top. Took [SHIP_TIME] to arrive."

`SHOT: 180° turn, full body, showing hem and sleeve length`

---

### 0:28–0:32 — Verdict + CTA

> "[PRICE]. I ordered two more in the same color. That's the review."

`ON-SCREEN: FEESBEES — Sage Minimal Tee`
`SHOT: shrug, half-smile, cut on the beat — no wave, no "link in bio" voiceover`

---

## Cut-down versions

**15s (Reels/Shorts):** Hook → 0:08–0:14 color turn → 0:14–0:18 weight proof → verdict.
**8s (paid social):** Hook → color turn → `[PRICE]` card.

## What makes this read as real

- Names an actual objection (silly brand name, thin product page) instead of pretending there isn't one
- Every claim is physical and demonstrable on camera — opacity, collar recovery, print texture
- Fit advice includes height and size, which is the single most-requested thing in tee reviews
- Ends flat. No sign-off, no discount code read. Sign-offs are the fastest tell that a video is an ad.
