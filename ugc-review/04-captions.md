# Captions & On-Screen Text

## Disclosure first

If the tee was gifted, paid, or commissioned, `#ad` or `#gifted` goes **at the
front of the caption** and on screen in the first 3 seconds. Not at the end of
the hashtag block — a disclosure nobody sees is not a disclosure. If the creator
bought it themselves with no relationship to the brand, say that instead, and
only if it's true.

## On-screen text cards

| Timing | Text | Style |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | `not sponsored, just weirdly obsessed` *(or `#ad`)* | small, top third, plain |
| 0:10 | `THIS COLOR ↗` | arrow pointing at the tee |
| 0:16 | `[GSM] — you can't see through it` | over the opacity shot |
| 0:24 | `[CREATOR_HEIGHT] · size [CREATOR_SIZE] · [FIT]` | over the 180° turn |
| 0:30 | `FEESBEES — Sage Minimal Tee · [PRICE]` | end card, 1.5s, no animation |

**Caption styling:** system sans, white, thin dark outline or subtle shadow. No
karaoke word-highlighting — it's the house style of every dropshipping ad on the
platform and viewers pattern-match it instantly.

## Comment replies (pre-written)

- **"is it see through?"** → "nope — there's a shot in the video of my hand behind it, nothing comes through. it's [GSM]."
- **"does it shrink?"** → "[SHRINK] — washed cold, hung dry, same size as day one."
- **"what size are you wearing"** → "[CREATOR_SIZE], I'm [CREATOR_HEIGHT]. size up if you want it looser."
- **"link?"** → "it's on their site, feesbees, sage minimal tee 🍵"
- **"is this an ad"** → answer honestly, immediately, in one line. Hedging here costs more trust than the disclosure ever would.
