# Shot List

**Kit:** phone, window, one person. That's the whole kit — a lit set kills the format.
**Settings:** 4K/60 if available (room for reframing and speed ramps), 9:16, HDR off.

---

## Setup

| | |
|---|---|
| **Light** | Large window, creator facing it, sun not direct. Sage green goes grey under warm indoor bulbs — shoot in daylight or the color story dies. |
| **Background** | Lived-in room. Some clutter. A styled backdrop reads as brand-produced. |
| **Camera** | Handheld, chest height. Slight drift is the point. No gimbal. |
| **Audio** | Phone mic at arm's length is fine and correct. Lav mics sound like ads. |
| **Wardrobe** | Neutral bottoms — white, cream, washed denim. Nothing that competes with the green. |

---

## Shots

**A — Talking head, tee worn.** Waist-up, creator slightly off-center. Covers hook, skepticism, verdict. *Shoot the full script here first, then pick up B–G as inserts.*

**B — Phone-in-hand.** Creator holds their phone showing the product page, tilted toward camera. 2s. Sells "I actually bought this."

**C — Window tilt.** Tee held on the hanger up to window light, slow tilt so the sage shifts warm→cool. 3s. **This is the money shot** — it's the only way the color reads truthfully on a compressed feed.

**D — Opacity proof.** Hand flat behind the chest panel, held to light. Hand should *not* show through. 2s. If it does show through, cut the shot and cut the weight claim.

**E — Collar macro.** Thumb running the ribbed collar, phone 15cm away, tap-to-focus locked. 2s.

**F — Print texture.** Fingernail flicked across the `FEESBEES` wordmark. 2s. Proves the print is soft-hand, not a stiff plastisol slab.

**G — 180° turn.** Full body, arms relaxed, slow turn. 3s. Hem, sleeve length, how the body falls. Non-negotiable — fit is the top comment on every tee review ever posted.

---

## Edit

- Cut on breath, not on silence. Trim the last 4–6 frames of every clip.
- Hard cuts only. No transitions, no zoom-punch on every line.
- Captions burned in, bottom third, high-contrast — most views are muted.
- Music low or absent. Trending audio under a review undercuts the review.
- Total: 30–35s. Past 40s the retention curve falls off a cliff.
- **Leave one flub in.** A stumble, a laugh, a re-start. It's the cheapest credibility you'll ever buy.
