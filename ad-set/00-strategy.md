# FEESBEES — Static Ad Set Strategy

10 static creatives, split across top and middle of funnel, designed to be
tested as **discrete variables** rather than as ten versions of the same idea.

---

## Brand read

Taken from the five product shots supplied:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mark** | Longhorn skull over an `FB` monogram, inside a distressed brush circle |
| **Lockup** | `FEESBEES` condensed slab + `est. 2019` rule |
| **Palette** | Black, bone white, sage, sand — plus a khaki/tan print colour on black |
| **Placements** | Left-chest hit, tonal sleeve hit, oversized front logo |
| **Two tiers** | *Badge* tees (full bull lockup) and *Minimal* tees (wordmark only) |
| **Register** | Western / working heritage. Understated. Not a loud streetwear brand. |

**Voice rule for every ad below:** flat, physical, unhurried. No exclamation
marks, no "obsessed", no "game-changer". The brand sells a uniform, not a drop.
The fastest way to make this brand look fake is to make it sound excited.

---

## Audience hypothesis

**Core:** men 25–45, western / ranch / outdoors / trades adjacent. Buys on
durability and fit, not novelty. Historically replaces the *same* shirt rather
than shopping for a new one — which is the single most important fact in this
whole document, because it means the winning offer is almost certainly a
**multi-pack**, not a first-purchase discount.

**Prospecting seeds:** western wear, ranch and livestock, hunting/fishing,
country music, trucks and towing, trade work, rodeo.

**Retargeting pools:** 30-day site visitors, add-to-cart no purchase, 50%+
video viewers, IG/FB engagers, past purchasers (for the rotation angle).

## Testing plan

**Round 1 — TOF, 6 creatives, one ad set, broad targeting.**
Let delivery pick. Budget for ~1,500 impressions per creative before judging.
Kill on **hook rate** (3-sec views ÷ impressions), not on ROAS — at this volume
ROAS is noise. Anything under a 20% hook rate is dead regardless of spend.

**Round 2 — MOF, 4 creatives, retargeting the pools above.**
Judge on cost per add-to-cart. Ad 09 and Ad 10 are the two most likely winners
and they answer different objections — *worth the money* vs *worth trusting* —
so run both before concluding.

**Round 3 — scale the winner's angle, not the winner's image.**
If 02 wins, the insight is *durability*, and the next three creatives should be
three more durability executions. Do not simply recolour the winner.

### What each ad isolates

Ads 01–06 deliberately vary one thing at a time so a result means something:

- **01 vs 03** — same aspiration, badge-forward vs lifestyle-forward
- **02 vs 09** — same durability argument, TOF emotional vs MOF rational
- **04 vs 10** — same multi-buy offer, cold vs warm
- **05** — the only text-led creative; if it wins on CTR but loses on CVR, that
  is a targeting signal, not a creative one

## ⚠️ Unverified claims — resolve before spend

Same gap flagged in `ugc-review/README.md`: the storefront is unreachable from
this environment, so no spec below is confirmed. Every one appears as a
bracketed placeholder in `01-ad-copy.md` and in the rendered creatives.

- `[GSM]` — fabric weight. **Ad 07 and Ad 09 do not work without this.**
- `[FABRIC]` — fibre content
- `[PRICE]` / `[BUNDLE_PRICE]` — retail and multi-pack pricing
- `[SIZES]`, `[FIT]` — size run and stated fit
- `[SHIP_TIME]`, `[RETURNS]` — dispatch window and returns policy

**Ad 08 and Ad 10 carry review placeholders.** They are laid out for real
customer reviews and must be filled from the actual review export. Do not ship
invented quotes — a fabricated testimonial is a false-advertising exposure, and
under the FTC's 2024 rule on fake reviews it carries civil penalties per
violation. If there is no review corpus yet, run Ads 01–07 and 09 and hold
these two back.
