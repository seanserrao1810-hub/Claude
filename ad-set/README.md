# FEESBEES — Static Ad Set (10 Creatives)

A paid-social ad set for the FEESBEES tee line: **6 top-of-funnel** creatives
for cold prospecting and **4 middle-of-funnel** creatives for retargeting.

All creatives were generated from the five real product photos supplied
(white badge tee, black badge tee, sage minimal, sand minimal, black
big-logo), passed to the image model as tagged references so colour, logo
artwork and placement carry through.

## ⚠️ Read before you spend money

**1. Every product spec is unconfirmed.** The storefront is unreachable from
this environment, so fabric weight, price, sizing, fit and returns all appear
as `[BRACKETS]` in the copy. **Ads 02, 07 and 09 are built on the fabric weight
and don't stand up without it.**

**2. Ads 08 and 10 are templates, not finished ads.** They render with visible
`PASTE REAL REVIEW HERE` placeholders on purpose. Fill them from your actual
review export, in the customer's own words. Invented testimonials are false
advertising, and the FTC's 2024 rule on fake and AI-generated reviews carries
civil penalties per violation. If there's no review corpus yet, run the other
eight and hold these back.

**3. The people in these creatives are AI-generated.** They're stand-ins for a
real shoot. They are not customers and must never be captioned as such.

**4. Check the wordmark on every creative before it goes live.** Image models
mangle small text. The logo, the `FEESBEES` wordmark and every headline need a
human read at 100% zoom — see the per-ad QA notes in `02-creatives.md`.

**5. Keep the Ad 09 comparison generic.** It compares against an unnamed
category average. Naming a competitor turns puffery into a claim you have to be
able to substantiate.
