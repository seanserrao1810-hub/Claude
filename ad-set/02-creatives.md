```

```

**This is not a finished ad.** It renders with a visible `PASTE REAL REVIEW
HERE` placeholder, deliberately. Drop in a genuine review from your export, in
the customer's own words, then set the name and city.

**Picking the quote:** the best-converting review is never the most enthusiastic
one. Take the one that names a **specific physical detail** — the collar after
washing, the weight, how it fits a real body — and ideally carries a small
reservation ("took a wash to soften up, then it was perfect"). Unqualified
praise reads as solicited. A small caveat reads as a person.

## Honest limitation — nobody has looked at these yet

**I generated these creatives but could not view them.** This container's egress
proxy blocks the image CDN in both directions, so the renders could not be
pulled back for inspection. Everything above describes what was *specified*, not
what was verified.

That makes the QA notes a checklist for you, not a report from me. Work through
them in this order:

1. **Every text string**, at 100% zoom. Image models mangle small type, and six
   of these ten creatives carry a headline that the ad doesn't work without.
2. **The bull lockup and the `FEESBEES` wordmark**, against the real garment.
   Ads 01, 04, 06 and 10 reproduce the logo largest and are most at risk.
3. **Garment identity** on the multi-product ads (04 and 10) — badge tees must
   keep the bull, minimal tees must keep the plain wordmark.
4. **Colour**, against the real product photos. Sage should read sage, not mint
   or olive. Sand should read warm, not grey.
5. **Hands and faces** on Ads 01, 03 and 08.

Anything that fails, tell me which ad and what's wrong and I'll re-run it — the
prompts are all recorded above, so a fix is a targeted regeneration rather than
a rebuild.

## If you'd rather set the type yourself

For the text-heavy creatives (05, 07, 09, 10), AI-rendered type will never be as
clean as real type. `build/` holds Oswald, Barlow Condensed, Archivo Black and
Inter as local webfonts, matching the condensed-caps direction used throughout.
Take the clean plates, set the headlines in Figma or Canva, and you'll get
sharper results than any prompt will produce.
